What MyCollegeCounsel is

MyCollegeCounsel is an educational companion for high-school seniors (and their families) applying to US colleges: a step-by-step curriculum through the whole admissions cycle, planning and tracking tools built on real, verified data, and an AI coach that helps you think — never one that does the work for you.

A guided journey

13 modules of short, honest lessons — building your list, essays, recommendations, the FAFSA, scholarships, and deciding — that adapt to where you’re starting from.

Tools of record

Discover 1,400+ real colleges with live federal data, track every application requirement and deadline on one calendar with reminders, and browse curated scholarships across all 50 states.

Coaching with boundaries

The AI coach brainstorms, tutors, and gives honest feedback. It never writes your essay, never files a form for you, and never takes your family’s financial numbers — that’s the whole point.
